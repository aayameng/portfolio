1. Hydrogen Atom Wavefunction

$$\Psi_{n,l,m}(r, \theta, \phi) = R_{n,l}(r) \cdot Y_l^m(\theta, \phi)$$

2. Radial Wavefunction $R_{n,l}(r)$

$$R_{n,l}(r) = \sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{n a_0}\right)^3 \frac{(n-l-1)!}{2n(n+l)!}} \cdot e^{-\rho/2} \cdot \rho^l \cdot L_{n-l-1}^{2l+1}(\rho) \quad \text{where } \rho = \frac{2r}{n a_0}$$

Where $L_{n-l-1}^{2l+1}(\rho)$ is the Associated Laguerre Polynomial.

3. Spherical Harmonics $Y_l^m(\theta, \phi)$

$$Y_l^m(\theta, \phi) = (-1)^m \sqrt{\frac{2l+1}{4\pi} \frac{(l-m)!}{(l+m)!}} \cdot P_l^m(\cos\theta) \cdot e^{i m \phi}$$

Where $P_l^m(\cos\theta)$ is the Associated Legendre Polynomial.
